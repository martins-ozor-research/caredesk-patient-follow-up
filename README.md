Caredesk – AI-Assisted Patient Follow-Up Prototype

 Overview

Caredesk is an early-stage patient follow-up prototype designed for pharmacies, clinics, hospitals and other healthcare facilities.

It explores how technology can support medication refill follow-up, appointment tracking and communication between healthcare providers and patients.

 The Problem

The idea came from my experience working in pharmacy practice.

I observed patients, particularly those managing chronic conditions, struggle with medication refill interruptions and forget important counselling instructions after leaving the pharmacy.

These experiences made me think about how technology could help healthcare providers maintain contact with patients beyond the point of dispensing.

 The Proposed Solution

Caredesk was conceptualised to explore a more structured approach to patient follow-up.

The prototype includes:

- Patient registration and record management.
- Medication refill reminder tracking.
- Follow-up message generation.
- Medication counselling information.
- Appointment management.
- Patient record search and printable summaries.

 Development Approach

I independently conceptualised and developed the prototype using Claude AI as an AI-assisted software development tool.

The project combines my pharmacy practice experience with my interest in digital health and technology-enabled healthcare solutions.

I demonstrated the prototype to five pharmacists and received initial feedback.

 Technology

- HTML
- CSS

 Current Development Stage

Caredesk is an early-stage prototype developed to demonstrate the concept and explore its potential.

It has not been clinically validated or deployed for patient care. Its features, security, usability and potential impact require further testing and evaluation.

 Future Research Possibilities

Potential areas for further exploration include:

- Digital interventions for medication adherence.
- Technology-supported patient follow-up in community pharmacies.
- Medication safety and continuity of care.
- Usability and acceptability of digital health tools.
- The role of AI-assisted tools in pharmaceutical care.
